# Planetary Frontiers

**Original 2D science-fiction action-adventure / platformer**

**HTML5 · CSS · JavaScript · Canvas · Web Audio API**

## Overview

**Planetary Frontiers** is an original 2D science-fiction action-adventure/platformer developed by Solegus under the SHONexus project umbrella.

Development began on **May 31, 2026**, with the first prototype build, v0.1.

The game follows **Nich Ozimov**, an Earth researcher from the year 2162. During an experimental transit through an Einstein–Rosen bridge, his research shuttle is thrown into an unknown region of the Milky Way and severely damaged by a meteor storm.

With limited energy and life-support resources, Nich must explore unfamiliar planets and artificial structures, recover technological components, establish local portals, restore access to his shuttle, and uncover the nature of the region into which the failed experiment has taken him.

## Current development state

The project is actively in development.

The current working branch has reached the **v0.8.8f** prototype generation and presently focuses on **Level 1 – Abandoned City Gate**, a four-stage opening world set on the ruined planet **NEX-17**.

The playable development build currently contains the first two major stages of Level 1. Stage 1-3 and Stage 1-4 are being designed as further parts of the same world, while design prototypes for subsequent levels and planets are also being developed, including their own environments, gameplay mechanics and distinctive features.

## Gameplay

Planetary Frontiers combines side-scrolling exploration, platforming, environmental interaction and combat.

The core gameplay includes:

- exploration of multi-stage planetary environments;
- platforming and traversal through hazardous areas;
- collection of technological artifacts and components;
- combat with autonomous machines and other hostile entities;
- environmental puzzles and mission-specific interactions;
- local portal activation and progression between areas;
- unlockable equipment capabilities;
- battles with varied bosses;
- narrative terminals, including simulations of retro computer interfaces;
- dialogue and environmental storytelling.

The long-term structure is designed to expand beyond linear stage progression into broader exploration between different planets and locations.

## Technology

The current version is developed as a self-contained browser game using:

- HTML5;
- CSS;
- JavaScript;
- Canvas 2D;
- Web Audio API.

The project currently emphasizes procedural generation and code-driven visual implementation, deliberately avoiding dependence on external sprite libraries.

Characters, enemies, environments, visual effects, interfaces and sound systems are prototyped directly in code.

Contemporary LLMs are also used as development tools for iterative coding, technical analysis, prototyping, and design exploration, alongside hands-on engineering work, testing, evaluation, and integration of the results.

## Procedural development

Planetary Frontiers is also used as an experimental environment for procedural game-development techniques.

Separate internal development laboratories have been created for areas such as:

- character rendering and animation;
- enemy visual design;
- level-layout analysis;
- interface systems;
- environmental backgrounds;
- procedural audio;
- interactive terminals and in-world computer systems.

Successful experimental systems are gradually integrated into the main game build.

## Level 1 – Abandoned City Gate

The first game world is **NEX-17**, home to the remnants of a ruined technological civilization and a still-active planetary AI. Its surface contains abandoned cities, damaged infrastructure, toxic areas, surviving autonomous machines, and traces of a catastrophic past.

The planned Level 1 structure consists of four stages:

**Stage 1-1** – the ruined city during sunset, introducing exploration, artifacts, environmental hazards, machines and basic mission systems.

**Stage 1-2** – the city at night, expanding combat, enemy behavior, environmental interaction and technological systems.

**Stage 1-3** – an industrial complex beneath the city, moving exploration into the surviving technological infrastructure of NEX-17.

**Stage 1-4** – a further descent into the history and surviving systems of the planet, including a major encounter connected to the civilization that once controlled the city.
